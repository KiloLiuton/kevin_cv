% Resumo
\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt} I am a scientist with a PhD in computational and statistical physics and solid
quantitative basis. I first started programming in Tcl/Tk during my masters degree for interfacing with various
electronic equipments and soon moved to Python, which I have been using ever since.

Inspired by advances in artificial intelligence in the form of AlphaGo, in my PhD I moved to statistical and
computational physics, implementing various dynamical systems simulations in C, leading me to a collaboration with the
University of Michigan, where I was able to give a mathematical description for some systems of oscillators which
corroborated simulation results.

This led me to a diverse experience in data science, machine learning and software development: In 2018 I joined Olivia
AI to develop sentiment analysis and a chatbot before the advent of LLM's. By 2020 I was hired at Rappi to develop life
time value models and user metrics to aid in the company's expansion in South America, and finally in 2021 I joined
Spacetime Labs (now Moray AI) to work with machine learning for precision agriculture and crop management
through climate and sensor data.
